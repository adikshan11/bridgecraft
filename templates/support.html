Connect Studio

  • Claude: on claude.ai, open Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector, paste {{ site_url }}/studio, and sign in. It then works in the Claude mobile apps too.
  • ChatGPT: turn on developer mode (Settings → Apps & Connectors → Advanced), add a connector with the same address, and sign in.

Then ask your assistant to list your Studio files, or to import one. Asking it to disconnect, or to close your account, deletes your files and your ArtCraft grant immediately. The same actions are POST /account/disconnect and POST /account/close with your connector token.
