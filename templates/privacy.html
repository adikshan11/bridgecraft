BridgeCraft is run by Adithya Shankaran as an independent open-source project. It is not operated by Storytold or the ArtCraft team. This policy covers ArtCraft Studio, the connector at {{ site_url }}/studio, and this website.

What we receive

  • An account ID. You sign in through our sign-in provider (Auth0). Studio receives only a unique ID for your account from it. We never see your password.
  • Your files. Files you upload or import, and the files Studio makes for you, such as edited videos, images, documents and signed PDFs.
  • Tool requests. When your AI assistant uses Studio, it sends the tool name and its inputs, such as a file ID, a caption or a page number. We don't receive your conversation.
  • Request logs. Our host (Render) records each web request's time, path, status and IP address to keep the service running. Logs do not contain file contents, tokens or secrets, and tool inputs are not logged.
  • Usage counts. To show on the status page how the service is used, we count requests by page (home, status, Studio and so on), tool calls by tool name and Craft app, whether each worked, and how long it took, added up by the hour. The counts have no paths, inputs, files, account IDs or IP addresses, and we delete them after 14 days.

How it's used

Only to do what you ask: store your files, run the edit or Craft app you asked for, and give your files back to you. We have no ads and no third-party analytics, set no tracking cookies, don't sell or share your data, and don't use it to train AI models.

Where it's kept and for how long

  • Files are kept in a private storage bucket (S3-compatible), under users/<id>/ for your account only. Other users can't list or open them.
  • Files are deleted {{ retention_days }} days after your session ends. Deleting a file, disconnecting, or closing your account deletes them immediately.
  • Sign-in uses a connector token for https://connect.getartcraft.com/mcp. Studio checks its issuer, signature, expiry, audience and scopes. That token is not sent to ArtCraft or to other APIs. Studio exchanges it with the sign-in service for a separate token, used only for generation and library reads, and stores that token encrypted until you disconnect, close the account, or the grant is revoked. It is not logged and is not sent back to your assistant.
  • While a tool runs, Studio copies the files it needs into a temporary folder on the server. The folder is deleted when the tool finishes, or when a Craft app session closes or has been idle for a few minutes.
  • Download links expire after one hour, and upload links after 15 minutes.

Other services involved

  • Your AI assistant (for example ChatGPT or Claude) handles your conversation under its own privacy policy.
  • Auth0 handles sign-in.
  • Render hosts the server, and a storage provider (Backblaze B2 or Cloudflare R2) keeps the files.
  • When you ask Studio to import a file from a web address, the server downloads it from that address. Only public https addresses are allowed.

Electronic signatures

When you ask Studio to sign a PDF, it adds the name or signature image you choose and an audit page containing the original document's SHA-256 hash. That signed PDF is stored like any other file of yours.

Your choices

You can delete your files at any time through the assistant. To have everything tied to your account removed, or to ask what we hold, contact us as described on the support page. Studio isn't meant for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top. The source code, including what gets stored, is public on GitHub.
